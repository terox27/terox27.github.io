Core Innovations

🧬

Surgical 1.58-Bit & Zero-Copy (.qcore)

Replaces heavy non-linear MLP layers with ternary {-1, 0, +1} matrices and heals quantization errors via adaptive Skeleton-DoRA rank slices. Loads gigabyte models into RAM in under 0.1s using memory-mapped zero-copy buffers.

🧠

4-Track Cognitive Memory Router

Intelligently segregates high-level reasoning and causal rules (forged into portable .spec modules) from factual entities, timestamps, and numbers (stored as instant < 1ms key-value records in local databases).

💤

Offline Sleep Consolidation (/sleep)

New knowledge is queued during conversations and consolidated in background sleep passes. Protected by automated Red-Team validation, canary prompts, and 3-generation rolling backups (/undo-sleep) for zero-risk rollbacks.

💎

Memory Crystals (KvCapsule)

Compresses lengthy documents, notes, and training traces into miniaturized memory crystals featuring 64-dimensional quantized semantic sketches and anchor facts that recall in under 1 millisecond.

📐

Coarse-to-Fine Concept Separation

Separates abstract syntactic concept skeletons from volatile surface details, enabling models to adapt logic patterns across multiple domains without catastrophic forgetting or memory bloat.

📦

Portable Specialists & SpecVault (msv)

Pack and trade domain specialists (.specp) across different model scales and platforms. Governed by a decentralized 3-zone privacy vault: Private Local, 256-bit Encrypted Team, and Inspected Public.

🛡️ 100% Free & Open Source — Local Privacy by Design

Merkaba01 is 100% open source under the permissive MIT license. There are no subscriptions, no token meters, no corporate telemetry, and no lock-ins. Everything runs locally and offline on your own machine. When you need recent external information, an optional zero-cost web lookup can be toggled on demand (and disabled anytime via /web off) — keeping you in total control of your data and code.

⚡ Modular Drop-in Hardware Acceleration (MPS-HW-1.0)

Merkaba01 features a clean-room hardware-agnostic core written in 100% pure Rust. Rather than locking users to specific proprietary runtimes, acceleration is organized into modular drop-in add-ons. Cross-platform support is rolling out progressively:

NVIDIA Acceleration CUDA Driver & PTX support for unified memory and dedicated RTX VRAM
Intel Architectures x86_64 AVX2/AVX-512 VNNI SIMD and Level-Zero / Vulkan Compute
